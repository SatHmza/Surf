Surfing, the way it should feel

Rabat Surf Club started as a few friends paddling out at Oudaya every morning before work. Now we teach anyone who wants in, total beginners welcome. No egos, no gear that makes you feel silly.

Just good waves, mint tea after, and a break that somehow never gets too crowded.

  • A-frame wavePeels both ways, gentle enough for a first lesson
  • Oct – MarPeak season, consistent Atlantic swell
  • FR / EN / ARInstruction in whichever language is easiest for you

A morning at Rabat Surf Club

07:00

First light over the Kasbah. The wind is still asleep and the wave peels clean and slow — the best hour of the day for a first lesson.

10:00

Boards waxed, wetsuits on. Groups paddle out together, coached in from the shoulder of the wave one at a time.

13:00

Out of the water, mint tea in hand, replaying the best wave of the morning with the people who just watched you catch it.

Find us at Oudaya Beach

Right where the Bou Regreg meets the Atlantic, beneath the walls of the Kasbah of the Udayas. The A-frame peak here breaks gently in both directions, making it one of the most forgiving beginner waves on this stretch of coast.
